## Research Collaborator

**Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST)** | CARE Laboratory 
*Japan (Remote) — October 2024 – December 2024*

---

### Project: Geometric Deep Learning for 3D Content Generation

Conducted a comprehensive literature survey on state-of-the-art Geometric Deep Learning approaches for generating 3D models in Virtual Reality environments.

### Key Contributions

- **Literature Survey**: Compiled a structured review of Geometric DL solutions for generating 3D models from multimodal inputs (text, images, point clouds)

- **Research Synthesis**: Analyzed and categorized existing approaches based on geometric representations, learning paradigms, and application domains

### Focus Areas

`Geometric Deep Learning` `3D Generation` `Virtual Reality` `Multimodal Learning` `Literature Review`
